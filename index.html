Pond Farm Brewing Co.

A full redesign of the brewery & taproom's website — from the ground up, not a template patch.

Role
Wix Designer, Photographer
Worked with
Founders & general manager
Scope
Site redesign, product & lifestyle photography, CMS & back-of-house tooling

Pond Farm needed a site that could keep up with a working brewery and taproom — menus that change, events that need promoting, merch and gift cards that need to sell online, all without pulling staff away from the floor to update a webpage.

The redesign covered the full site: structure, visual design, and original product and lifestyle photography shot on location. Just as important was what happens after launch — a CMS setup and back-of-house workflow the team could actually run themselves, day to day, without calling a designer every time the kitchen hours change.

UCSF Liver & Kidney Health Expo

A zero-to-one website and registration system for UCSF's donor health expo — built to feel credible enough for a hospital system without losing its warmth.

Role
Wix Designer
Worked with
Event team, surgeons & clinicians
Scope
Zero-to-one Wix site, brand design, registration form system

UCSF's Center for Living Organ Donation needed a site built from scratch for its annual Liver & Kidney Health Expo — a public event mixing clinical education with real community outreach, aimed at donors, transplant candidates, families, and the surgeons and clinicians running it.

The build covered everything from brand and visual design to a full registration system: event sign-up, a free liver health screening with limited capacity, and eligibility questionnaires for kidney and liver donation. The goal was a site that reads as credible as the hospital system behind it, without losing the warmth of an event built around real acts of generosity.
