For Companies That Work With Local Government

Built for the businesses that win local government contracts.

San Rafael Sites builds websites for contractors and consultants who bid on local government contracts with the City of San Rafael, County of Marin, and other Bay Area agencies — so your capabilities, past work, and certifications are easy to find and easy to trust.

Get in touch

Why it matters

Before an agency picks up the phone, someone on the evaluation committee is checking your website — looking for proof you can do the work: licenses, bonding and insurance, DBE/SBE/WBE certifications, and examples of projects you've completed for other agencies. A site that puts that proof front and center can be the difference between a callback and a pass.

What these sites need to do

  1. 01

    Capability statements & past performance

    Evaluators want specific project types, project sizes, and results — not a generic "about us." Your past work should read the way an RFP evaluation matrix reads.

  2. 02

    Licenses, bonding & certifications

    DBE/SBE/WBE status, contractor license numbers, and proof of insurance and bonding capacity should be easy to find — not buried in a PDF nobody opens.

  3. 03

    Built to be found

    When a project manager or purchasing agent searches for a contractor in Marin County, your site should be the one that shows up — not just the one you hand out on a business card.
