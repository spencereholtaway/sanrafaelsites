Why it matters

Wix makes it easy to get a website live — but easy to launch doesn't always mean easy to love. Spacing that's off on mobile, images that crop wrong, a title tag that still says "Home," headings that skip around, no alt text anywhere — these are the small things that quietly hold a DIY site back, both in how it looks and in whether Google can find it. A tune-up fixes them without touching what already works.

What a tune-up covers

  1. 01

    Design clean-up

    Tightening inconsistent spacing, fixing mismatched fonts and colors, and cleaning up the sections that still look like a template — so the site feels intentional and matches how you actually want to be perceived.

  2. 02

    Responsive & mobile fixes

    Going through every page on phone, tablet, and desktop and fixing the overlapping text, oversized images, and broken layouts that Wix's separate mobile editor tends to let slip through.

  3. 03

    SEO clean-up & optimization

    Page titles, meta descriptions, header structure, image alt text, page speed, and getting your sitemap into Google Search Console — the fundamentals that decide whether people searching for a business like yours can actually find you.

Common questions

Wix tune-up, answered

Can someone fix my Wix website without rebuilding it?

Yes. A tune-up works inside the site you already built — cleaning up design, fixing layout and mobile issues, and improving SEO — without starting over on a new platform or a full redesign.

Why isn't my Wix website showing up on Google?

Usually it's a handful of missing basics: page titles and meta descriptions that were never set, images without alt text, a heading structure that skips levels, slow-loading pages, or a sitemap that was never submitted to Google Search Console. These are common on DIY Wix sites and are straightforward to fix.

Why does my Wix site look broken or misaligned on mobile?

Wix lets you design desktop and mobile layouts separately, and it's easy for the mobile version to drift out of sync as you add sections over time — overlapping text, oversized images, awkward spacing. A tune-up goes through every page on phone, tablet, and desktop and fixes what's broken.

How much does a Wix website tune-up cost?

It depends on how many pages your site has and what shape it's in — a small single-page site needs less work than a ten-page site with several broken sections. Send over your site and we'll take a look and quote a flat price before any work starts.

Do you redesign Wix websites, or only fix small issues?

Both — a tune-up can be as light as fixing mobile spacing and SEO basics, or as involved as reworking the visual design section by section. We scope it to what your site actually needs after reviewing it.

Can you improve my Wix site's SEO without moving off Wix?

Yes. Wix supports the SEO fundamentals — title tags, meta descriptions, alt text, structured data, sitemaps — they just aren't set up correctly on most self-built sites. We clean those up directly inside your existing Wix site.
